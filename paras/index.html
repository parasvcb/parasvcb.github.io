Research trajectory

My early work examined individual protein systems through molecular dynamics and residue-network analysis. During my Ph.D., I developed ENACT and ENACTdb to classify exon variation and its protein-coding consequences across transcripts. I later contributed to PEXMap, which assigns MS* peptides to exons and splice junctions. At Plaksha, I study population-stratified BRCA1/2 variation and contribute to ALS* transcriptomic analyses using explainable ML.

ENACT and ENACTdb

I developed ENACT to classify exon variation across all protein-coding transcripts of a gene. It distinguishes changes in exon inclusion, splice sites and translation—including dual exons that contribute to CDS* in one transcript and UTR* in another. ENACTdb adds protein domains, predicted SS* and IDRs* across five species.

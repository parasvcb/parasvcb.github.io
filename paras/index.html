Research focus

Protein variation in its biological context

A difference in RNA does not always produce a different protein, and a sequence change does not by itself establish a functional effect. I connect transcript differences with protein features and structural models to distinguish these possibilities.

My current work includes population-aware interpretation of BRCA variants and the diversification of cell-surface proteins. I am developing this work toward DNA-damage repair and cellular state in breast cancer. I also contribute to collaborative analyses in amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ALS).

Approach
I compare genetic variants, exon use and transcript sequences and map their differences onto protein features. Molecular dynamics and experimental collaboration help interpret the relevant structural states. I am also reading single-cell and spatial studies in preparation for planned analyses.
Core and extensionsProtein systems, transcript diversity and the development of my current work. Cancer and agentic AIPerspectives on cellular context and biological representation.

ENACT and ENACTdb · 2019–2025

Exon nomenclature and classification of transcripts

Focus. Distinguishing transcript differences by their predicted protein consequences.

Contribution. I developed the ENACT framework to classify exon attributes from a gene-centered view and map transcript changes to coding regions, amino-acid sequence changes, splice-site variation, and protein features.

Scientific use. ENACTdb associates exon variation with coding sequence and protein features, allowing transcript differences to be evaluated individually.

  • Annotated protein-coding genes across C. elegans, D. melanogaster, D. rerio, M. musculus, and H. sapiens.
  • Linked exon-level classes with coding status, amino-acid changes, splice-site shifts, domains, disordered regions, motifs, and related feature tracks.
  • Published the framework in Genome Research and the database in Bioinformatics Advances.

Molecular dynamics · 2020

Mutation-linked dynamics in Vibrio cholerae cytolysin

Focus. The effects of a hinge-region mutation on pore formation in a multidomain toxin.

Contribution. We used molecular dynamics and structural network analysis to compare wild-type VCC with the Y321A mutant and track how the mutation changed residue motions and domain contacts.

Scientific use. The analysis linked a local hydrophobic-pocket perturbation to domain communication needed for pore formation.

Publication

Steered molecular dynamics · 2021

Sequence design and mechanical failure in silk beta-sheet nanocrystals

Focus. Sequence and packing features that influence fracture under force.

Contribution. We compared natural silk nanocrystals with in silico beta-sheet models inspired by globular proteins and followed fracture using constant-velocity pulling simulations.

Scientific use. The work relates sequence and packing choices to mechanical failure, which can guide the design of silk-like biomaterials.

Methods

Transcriptomics and annotation

Build and compare transcript models from GTF/GFF annotations, RefSeq and Ensembl-style records, ORF mapping, coding-region mapping, and exon classification workflows.

Proteoform interpretation

Map transcript changes to amino-acid sequence, domains, intrinsically disordered regions, motifs, localization-relevant segments, splice-site shifts, and other protein features.

Structural biology and MD

Use molecular dynamics, steered MD, contact maps, residue networks, PCA, centrality, and community analysis to study motions, contacts, and mutation effects in proteins.

Programming and web resources

I build analysis scripts and web resources that help researchers work with biological data. My tools include Python and R for analysis, and JavaScript, React, Django, and MySQL for web development.
